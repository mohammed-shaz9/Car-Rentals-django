About NovaFleet

Our Story

Founded in 2012 and digitally expanded in 2018, NovaFleet has grown from a small fleet of 5 cars to one of Hyderabad's most trusted car rental services. With over a decade in business, we have served more than 15,000 happy customers.

Our mission is simple: provide top-notch, hassle-free car rentals with complete transparency. Every vehicle in our fleet undergoes rigorous maintenance — serviced every two weeks and professionally sanitized before each rental. We offer flexible rental plans, competitive pricing, and 24/7 roadside assistance.

Whether you need a compact hatchback for city commutes, a spacious SUV for family trips, or a luxury sedan for business travel — NovaFleet has you covered.
